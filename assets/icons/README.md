# Congregation Manager icon

`congregation_manager_icon.png` is the master artwork, generated using the
built-in image generation tool. It uses a simple community symbol in white
and teal on an indigo background.

The current platform assets are derived from this master:

- Android: `android/app/src/main/res/mipmap-*/ic_launcher.png`, at 48, 72,
  96, 144, and 192 pixels.
- iOS: `ios/Runner/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/`, sized according to
  its `Contents.json`, without an alpha channel.
- Windows: `windows/runner/resources/app_icon.ico`.
- Favicons: `favicon.ico` and
  `backend/CongregationManager.Server/wwwroot/favicon.ico`.

All paths above are relative to the repository root. Each ICO contains 16,
24, 32, 48, 64, 128, and 256 pixel PNG frames. The previous artwork and
temporary variant files have been removed.

## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished, very simple square app icon for Congregation Manager, a congregation organization app. A bold geometric community symbol: three small circular heads above one unified, softly rounded shoulder silhouette, the central person subtly emphasized in teal and the other forms white. Flat solid deep indigo background extending to all four edges. Center the compact mark with generous even padding, approximately 65 percent of the canvas width. Calm, friendly, professional, precise balanced geometry, crisp vector-like edges, extremely legible at favicon size. Use only deep indigo, white, and teal. No text, letters, checkmarks, documents, gradients, shadows, outlines, texture, 3D effects, mockups, border, or extra decoration. Deliver a single finished icon, straight-on, square 1024 by 1024.

## Final refinement prompt

Refine this app icon. Preserve the same simple three-person community symbol and its centered composition. Replace the entire background with a fully opaque, uniform deep indigo #3730A3 square extending edge to edge. Remove ALL glow, shadows, halos, speckles, texture and gradients around and inside the figures. The two rear people should be perfectly solid white; the front central person perfectly solid teal #14B8A6. Use precise crisp smooth geometric edges and clean separation between heads and shoulders. Flat vector-like professional app icon. No transparency anywhere, no black background, no text, no border, no mockup. Output one finished square raster image.
